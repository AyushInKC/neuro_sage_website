# NeuroSage — Product Overview

## Canonical description

NeuroSage is a mental-wellness awareness and self-care platform that brings together educational awareness content, a guided focus workspace, a reflective AI companion, and community-oriented wellness initiatives for students and young adults.

**Brand tagline:** “Be kind to your mind.”

**Related campaign line:** Brainwell Camp — “Revive your mind.”

NeuroSage is designed to make wellbeing and focus support feel approachable, warm, practical, and non-judgmental. The product encourages small, manageable steps rather than pressure to be constantly productive.

## Who it is for

The repository primarily speaks to:

- University and college students.
- Young adults navigating academic stress, overwhelming tasks, distraction, and everyday emotional strain.
- People curious about mental-health and neurodiversity awareness.
- Users who want a simple focus routine, a supportive reflective conversation, or information about a community wellness initiative.

Do not say it is exclusively for one diagnosis or that users must have a diagnosed condition to benefit. The product is positioned around awareness, reflection, and daily support rather than diagnosis.

## The problem it tries to address

The product's presentation identifies several common friction points:

- Mental-health topics can be hard to talk about and are often misunderstood.
- Large tasks can feel difficult to begin or break down.
- Students may feel pressure around exams, college, deadlines, and burnout.
- People may want a gentle place to reflect without being judged.
- Campus wellness efforts benefit from accessible information and community participation.

NeuroSage offers small tools and clear information around these situations. It does not claim to cure or clinically treat mental-health conditions.

## Product pillars

### 1. Awareness and education

A public-facing guide introduces ten psychological or mental-health-related topics: ADHD, phobias, depression, PTSD, OCD, bipolar disorder, insomnia, schizophrenia, paranoia, and panic disorder. The page presents descriptions and examples of common signs, with an explicit warning that awareness content is not a diagnosis.

### 2. Guided focus / Body Doubling Workspace

An authenticated workspace combines a configurable focus timer, user-defined intention, manageable checklist, browser-generated ambient sound options, box-breathing exercise, and a reflection note. Its purpose is to make starting and sustaining a work block feel more manageable.

### 3. AI Wellness Companion

An authenticated chat experience provides warm reflection, grounding suggestions, and supportive self-care prompts. The current server code calls a locally running Ollama model and includes a rule-based fallback if Ollama is unavailable. The service includes a simple keyword-based crisis response pathway. It is not a therapist, doctor, diagnostic tool, or replacement for professional care.

### 4. Brainwell Camp

A featured wellness initiative described as a space to pause, reconnect, build mindful habits, and participate in supportive conversations. The registration application supports student/institution details, a ₹49 fee configured in the current code, payment-slip upload, a numbered queue, payment verification, seat allocation, and waitlisting. The displayed event date and venue are “Details to be announced” by default; do not invent them.

### 5. Live Body Doubling

A separate paid option is listed at ₹299 in the current code. After submitting a UPI transaction reference and receiving manual administrator approval, an eligible user can join an active Jitsi-based session. The live page places a video call beside the user's task checklist. The repository does not show automatic payment-gateway verification.

### 6. Future resources

The user interface presents a Wellness Pharmacy and Mental Wellness Professionals directory as coming soon. The Books for the Mind page currently displays a curated reading list, although the README also groups the mind library with future services. Represent book-library readiness cautiously: books are displayed in the current UI code, but current content quality, rights, and operational status are not verified. Pharmacy sales and professional appointments are not currently represented as available end-to-end services.

## What makes the product feel different

Grounded differentiators in the current experience include:

- A soft, calm visual identity rather than a high-pressure productivity dashboard.
- Self-paced tools that combine focus structure, breathing, and reflection in one workspace.
- A product-level emphasis on awareness and non-diagnostic support.
- Community initiatives through Brainwell Camp and facilitator-led live body doubling.
- Transparent status language for payments and camp registration.

Do not claim these are objectively better than every competitor. Say they are the features and design choices NeuroSage emphasizes.

## What NeuroSage is not

- Not an emergency response service.
- Not a mental-health diagnosis or screening service.
- Not a replacement for a qualified clinician, therapy, or psychiatric care.
- Not evidence that someone has or does not have a condition based on the awareness page.
- Not an automatic payment-processing provider for the documented UPI QR flows.
- Not a currently available online pharmacy or bookable clinician directory, based on the inspected repository.

## One-sentence version

NeuroSage is a calm, student-oriented mental-wellness platform combining awareness education, practical focus tools, supportive AI reflection, and community wellness initiatives.
