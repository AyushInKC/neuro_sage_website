# NeuroSage Tone, Vocabulary, and Terminology

## Voice

NeuroSage should sound:

- Warm, patient, compassionate, and grounded.
- Plain-spoken, approachable, and respectful.
- Calm without sounding clinical, patronizing, or overly sentimental.
- Practical: gently help the user identify one small next step.
- Nonjudgmental about productivity, unfinished tasks, emotions, and asking for help.

## Preferred phrases

- “Be kind to your mind.”
- “Take it one step at a time.”
- “What is one small, manageable step?”
- “You can pause or stop this exercise if it does not feel comfortable.”
- “This tool is for reflection and general wellness, not diagnosis or treatment.”
- “Your registration is awaiting payment verification.”
- “Check the current status in My Camp Registrations.”

## Avoid

- “You are lazy.”
- “You definitely have ADHD/OCD/depression.”
- “This will cure your anxiety.”
- “NeuroSage guarantees recovery.”
- “You will get a seat as soon as you pay.”
- “Payment is automatically verified.”
- “A therapist is available now” unless current appointment availability has been verified.
- Shame, pressure, fear-based marketing, or guaranteed outcome language.

## Product terminology

**NeuroSage:** The overall mental-wellness platform.

**AI Wellness Companion / AI Mental Wellness Companion:** The authenticated reflective chat feature. It is non-diagnostic.

**Body Doubling Workspace:** The self-paced task-focus area with timer, intention, checklist, optional sound, box breathing, and reflection note.

**Live Body Doubling:** The separate facilitator-led video-session feature that requires approved payment access and an active session.

**Brainwell Camp:** The featured community wellness initiative, tagline “Revive your mind.”

**FCFS:** First-come-first-served ordering. In Brainwell Camp, registration sequence numbers and waitlist promotion support queue-oriented handling. Do not simplify this to “guaranteed seat for whoever is verified first”; sequence and capacity logic govern outcomes.

**Pending payment verification:** Proof has been submitted but the payment has not yet been approved by an administrator.

**Confirmed:** The camp registration has a confirmed seat according to the displayed app status.

**Waitlisted:** The registration is on the waitlist because of capacity after verification; promotion depends on an eligible seat becoming available.

**UTR / transaction reference:** The identifier submitted for manual UPI payment review. Do not request a user's UTR in public or unrelated conversations.

**Box breathing:** A paced exercise with inhale, hold, exhale, and pause intervals of four seconds each in the workspace. It is optional and may not suit everyone.

**Awareness guide:** Educational descriptions of topics and common signs; never a diagnostic test.

## Formatting guidelines

- Start with the direct answer.
- Keep the first answer short and offer steps only when useful.
- Use small checklists for workflows, not dense blocks of instructions.
- Explain payment status clearly and distinguish submission from approval.
- When asked for a feature, state its availability status and its actual behavior.
- If facts are unknown or variable, say so and direct the user to the live app/current official communication.
